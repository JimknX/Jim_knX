// ABOUT

Jim knX, born Kwandiokuhle Jim, is a Pretoria-based artist, producer, and creative originally from the Eastern Cape.

Born in Cradock in 2006, Jim moved to King William’s Town at the age of eight, where his passion for becoming an artist began. In 2017, he relocated to Pretoria, where exposure to mainstream music inspired him to begin producing and recording music using mobile platforms such as FL Studio Mobile, BandLab, and Voloco.

In 2021, Jim knX began pursuing music more seriously with the support of a friend and artist known as McGeorge.YNL. He officially released his debut single, The Surge (So Fika), in 2024.

Jim knX went on to release Pitori Symptoms Pt. 1—a project shaped by the influence, culture, and experiences of Pretoria. Now, Jim knX continues to develop his sound and identity.
